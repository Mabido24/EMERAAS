Legal & Police Assistance

Navigating legal issues, arrests, police interrogations, or contract disputes in a foreign country can be overwhelming, especially with language barriers and different procedural laws. EMERAAS provides immediate, professional legal guidance to protect your rights.

How Emeraas Helps You

⚖️ 24/7 Telephone Legal Counseling

In case of arrest or detention by police, our response center connects you immediately with qualified legal advisors who explain your situation and options under Thai law.

🤝 Local Bail & Court Coordination

If you are detained or facing formal charges, we coordinate with local bail lawyers and embassy officials to expedite your release and manage the necessary paperwork.

👮 Active Police Station Liaison

If you need to make a police report or are called in for questioning, we ensure a representative helps translate and protect your statement.

Legal Emergency Advisory

Do not sign any police report or official statement written in Thai unless our legal coordinators or your embassy's representative has reviewed and translated it for you. Call us at +66 (0) 2 113 1200 immediately.
