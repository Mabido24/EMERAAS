Помощь при госпитализации

In Thailand, private hospitals require confirmation of payment guarantee (either cash, credit card, or primary travel insurance approval) before initiating emergency treatment or admitting you to a ward. This can cause critical delays during accidents or severe health crises.

How Emeraas Helps You

💳 Fast-Track Admission Guarantee

We work directly with hospital registration desks to verify your Emeraas membership and coordinate billing options with international travel insurance providers, ensuring you receive treatment without delay.

👩 Medical Translation Desk

Medical terminology in Thai can be extremely difficult to grasp. Our bilingual desk helps translate symptoms, treatments, and doctors' diagnoses accurately.

📞 Family Notification

If you are unable to contact your family back home, our desk notifies your emergency contact list and coordinate updates with your embassy.

Hospital Registration Tip

Always present your digital or printed Emeraas Emergency Card alongside your passport at the hospital lobby. The admission team can scan the QR code to dial our emergency verification desk.
