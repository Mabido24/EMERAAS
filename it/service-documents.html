Documenti e oggetti smarriti

Losing your passport, driver's license, credit cards, or keys in a foreign country can be highly disruptive. EMERAAS guides you through the recovery and replacement process step-by-step, removing administrative hurdles.

How Emeraas Helps You

🚓 Police Report Assistance

Before replacing any official document, you must file a report at a local police station. We assist you in drafting the report in Thai and accompany you if needed.

🏢 Embassy Coordination

We connect you directly with your national embassy or consulate in Thailand to fast-track emergency passport issuance and travel letters.

💳 Card Block & Key Coordination

If you lose your wallet or keys, we assist in contact coordination with local banks to freeze your cards and locate locksmiths or recovery agents.

Emergency Document Tip

Always keep digital copies of your passport page, visa stamps, and entry stamps securely stored on your email or cloud storage. This makes the replacement process 10 times faster!
