道路交通事故协助

Road traffic incidents are among the most common emergencies faced by expats and tourists in Thailand. A minor collision can escalate quickly if there is a language barrier or if you do not understand local reporting procedures.

How Emeraas Helps You

📞 24/7 Telephone Hotline Translation

Our response team acts as a telephone translator on the spot, explaining the situation to local drivers and emergency responders.

👮 Traffic Police Liaison

Dealing with Thai traffic police requires tact and clarity. We liaise with the authorities to ensure the incident report is accurate and your rights are respected.

🚑 Medical Coordination

If there are injuries, we coordinate with local rescue services and fast-track hospital transport.

Immediate SOS Steps

If you are in an accident, do not sign any documents in Thai that you do not understand. Dial our emergency hotline at +66 (0) 2 113 1200 immediately.
